# Code of Conduct

NudgeKit is an open-source project that welcomes contributors from everywhere. We want this to be a place where good engineering and good manners are the default.

## The short version

- **Be respectful.** Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- **Assume good intent.** Most people are trying to help.
- **Keep it technical.** Critique code, not character.
- **No harassment, ever.** Personal attacks, slurs, doxxing, sexualized comments, and similar behavior are not tolerated.

## Where this applies

- GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and code review.
- Any other space where you are representing the project (chats, conferences, etc.).

## Examples of behavior we want

- Polite, specific bug reports and feature requests.
- Honest, kind code review — including when the answer is "no" or "not yet".
- Crediting prior work and giving reviewers time to respond.
- Helping new contributors find their way around the codebase.

## Examples of behavior we do not want

- Insults, name-calling, or condescending language.
- Harassment in public or private — including unwanted contact after someone has asked you to stop.
- Discrimination on the basis of identity, background, or experience level.
- Posting other people's private information without their consent.
- Sustained, off-topic disruption of issues, PRs, or discussions.

## Reporting

If you witness or experience behavior that violates this code, please report it privately by opening a private GitHub Security Advisory (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)) and titling it `Code of Conduct: contact`. The maintainers will follow up.

Do not name accused individuals in public threads. We want to investigate fairly.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take any action they consider appropriate, including:

- A private warning.
- A public correction in a thread.
- Editing, removing, or hiding comments, commits, or other contributions.
- Temporarily or permanently banning a contributor from the project.

Decisions are made by the maintainers and are not subject to debate in public threads. We will explain our reasoning to the parties involved.
